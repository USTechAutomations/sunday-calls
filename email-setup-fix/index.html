Email setup fix

Check your domain's three email sender settings

Type your web address. In a few seconds you see whether SPF, DKIM and DMARC look right. Mail services use these three public settings to tell your real mail from fakes. The check is free.

What you get

The exact two lines to paste for SPF and DMARC, and click-by-click steps for DKIM, which only your mail provider can create. Plus a private result page: each time you open it, it looks up your public records and shows whether our two lines are in place. We look at all three settings again 14 days after you pay and show what we find on that page. Getting these settings right helps your mail get accepted, but it does not by itself guarantee the inbox.

The two prices

$49 for the fix. $99 for the fix plus a second look at your settings 30 days later. Nothing else is charged.

How it works

  1. Run the free check above. If there is something to fix, tick the other services that send mail for you.
  2. Pick $49 or $99 and pay.
  3. You land on a private result page, usually ready within 30 minutes, 24 hours at most. Bookmark it; we do not email it. Lost the link? House rules

This is automated: a program does the work and a second program tests it. It is built with AI, and no person reads your order. We never ask for a password or login; you paste the lines in yourself at your domain provider. The free check runs in your own browser and looks up public records through two public lookup services (Cloudflare and Google). What you type into it is not sent to us. We do count visits to this page.

Money back

You get your money back if you put our two lines in unchanged, followed our steps for the third setting, and any of the three still shows not valid 14 days later. A buyer who never puts them in is not covered. You also get it back if, after you paid, we find nothing to fix or cannot do the job. Refunds are made by a person at the company, not by the machine. To ask for one, use the contact in the footer.
