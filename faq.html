Questions about the pre-order

Plain answers. If we do not know, the answer says UNKNOWN or says the step is not set up yet.

Who is the book for?

An adult who wants a parent's or grandparent's stories kept. The storyteller is that parent or grandparent.

Does the storyteller have to type?

No. They do not type, install an app, or open email. They answer the phone.

Who makes the calls?

An AI interviewer makes the calls. It is not a person.

Are the calls recorded?

Yes. The call is recorded so we can write the book. The storyteller is told at the start and can say no. We never call again if they say no. Recordings belong to the family. We delete on request. A contact address for that request is not published yet.

When does the book ship?

We phone the storyteller within 7 days of the test closing. The test closes 30 days after the page goes live. Then: first books ship about 12 weeks after your storyteller's first call.

What if the pre-order does not go ahead?

if we do not reach enough pre-orders to start, every pre-order is refunded in full. Enough means at least 10 pre-orders in the 30 days after the page goes live. Fewer than 3, and we refund every pre-order and close. If the count is 3 to 9, we decide and tell buyers.
