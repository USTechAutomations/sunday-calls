EPA 608 practice test: what each part covers and how to practise

The Section 608 test is the exam a technician passes to earn Section 608 certification. This page explains what each of the four parts covers and how to practise one part at a time. Every fact below shows EPA's exact words and a link to the official source.

What the test is for

Technicians who maintain, service, repair, or dispose of equipment that could release refrigerants into the atmosphere must be certified.

EPA's page says: "technicians who maintain, service, repair, or dispose of equipment that could release refrigerants into the atmosphere must be certified"

Read it on the official source

To be certified a technician must pass an EPA-approved test, and the tests differ by equipment type.

EPA's page says: "Technicians are required to pass an EPA-approved test to earn Section 608 Technician Certification. The tests are specific to the type of equipment the technician seeks to work on."

Read it on the official source

The four parts

Every test has a Core part. On top of Core you sit the part for the equipment you want to work on: Type 1, Type 2 or Type 3. Here is how EPA describes each one.

Group I asks questions on the environmental impact of CFCs, HCFCs, and substitute refrigerants, on laws and regulations, and on the changing industry outlook.

The federal rule says: "Group I will ask questions in the following areas: 1. Environmental impact of CFCs, HCFCs, and substitute refrigerants 2. Laws and regulations 3. Changing industry outlook"

Read it on the official source

EPA describes Type I certification as the one for servicing small appliances.

EPA's page says: "For servicing small appliances (Type I)."

Read it on the official source

The page describes Type II as for servicing or disposing of high- or very high-pressure appliances, except small appliances and motor-vehicle air conditioners.

EPA's page says: "For servicing or disposing of high- or very high-pressure appliances, except small appliances and MVACs (Type II)."

Read it on the official source

EPA describes Type III as the certification for servicing or disposing of low-pressure appliances.

EPA's page says: "For servicing or disposing of low-pressure appliances (Type III)."

Read it on the official source

EPA's topic lists

EPA publishes a list of topics for each part. These are the Core topics:

  • Environmental Impacts
  • Clean Air Act and Montreal Protocol
  • Section 608 Regulations
  • Substitute Refrigerants and oils
  • Refrigeration
  • Three R Definitions
  • Recovery Techniques
  • Dehydration Evacuation
  • Safety
  • Shipping

And these are the Type 2 topics:

  • Leak Detection
  • Leak repair requirements
  • Recovery Techniques
  • Recovery Requirements
  • Refrigeration
  • Safety

Type 1 and Type 3 have their own lists. All four are on EPA's test topics page.

How to practise one part at a time

  1. Pick one part. Do Core first, because every test includes it.
  2. Read EPA's topic list for that part and mark the topics you cannot explain out loud.
  3. Answer a short set of questions with your books closed. The real test is closed-book, so practise that way.
  4. Read the explanation for every answer, right or wrong. The reason matters more than the letter.
  5. Write down the topics you missed and study those before the next set.
  6. Come back to the part a few days later and try a fresh set.

Type II and Type III tests must be closed-book, proctored, and given in a secure setting.

The federal rule says: "Technicians must pass a closed-book, proctored test, administered in a secure environment, by an EPA-approved certifying program to be certified as a Type II or Type III technician."

Read it on the official source

The passing score for the closed-book Type I, II, III and Universal tests is 70 percent.

The federal rule says: "The passing score for the closed-book Type I, Type II, Type III and Universal certification test is 70 percent."

Read it on the official source

Three free sample questions

1. Who gives the Section 608 technician certification test?

  • A. EPA staff at a regional EPA office
  • B. A certification program on EPA's approved list
  • C. Your state's contractor licensing board
  • D. Your employer's safety officer
Show the answer

Answer: B. EPA publishes a list of certification programs and says: "Programs on this list are approved to provide the EPA Section 608 technician certification test." Source: https://www.epa.gov/section608/certification-programs-section-608-technicians (fetched 2026-09-29).

2. Which statement about the certification card is true?

  • A. EPA hands you the card at the test site
  • B. EPA mails the card within 30 days
  • C. EPA does not issue certification cards
  • D. EPA issues cards only to Universal technicians
Show the answer

Answer: C. EPA's test information page says: "EPA does not issue certification cards." Source: https://www.epa.gov/section608/section-608-technician-certification-0 (fetched 2026-09-29).

3. On EPA's published topic list, which topic appears for Type 3 (low-pressure) but not for Type 2 (high-pressure)?

  • A. Leak Detection
  • B. Recharging Techniques
  • C. Safety
  • D. Recovery Requirements
Show the answer

Answer: B. EPA lists Leak Detection, Safety and Recovery Requirements under both parts; Recharging Techniques is listed only under Type 3. Source: https://www.epa.gov/section608/test-topics (fetched 2026-09-29).
