Questions about the Gauge Drills EPA 608 practice pack

What is in the pack?

240 original practice questions in four parts: Core 100, Type 1 40, Type 2 80 and Type 3 20. Type 3 is the smallest part because EPA's public pages and the federal rule say less about low-pressure equipment, and we only write a question when an official quote backs the answer. Every answer has a plain explanation and a link to the official EPA page or federal rule it comes from. You also get a one-page rules sheet for each part, and practice sets of up to 25 with your score by part and by topic.

Are these the questions on the real exam?

No. We wrote every question ourselves, so none of them is a question from the real exam. EPA keeps its own question bank private.

EPA keeps the real question bank private and gives it only to approved certifying programs, so the real question pool is not public.

The federal rule says: "The Administrator will release this bank of questions only to approved technician certification programs."
