How the EPA 608 test is given: who runs it, online or in person, and your card

EPA writes the rules for the Section 608 test, but it does not give the test itself. This page explains who does, whether you can take it online, and what happens afterwards. Every fact shows EPA's exact words and a link.

Who runs the test

The test must be given by an EPA-approved certifying organization.

EPA's page says: "Tests must be administered by an EPA-approved certifying organization."

Read it on the official source

To be certified a technician must pass an EPA-approved test, and the tests differ by equipment type.

EPA's page says: "Technicians are required to pass an EPA-approved test to earn Section 608 Technician Certification. The tests are specific to the type of equipment the technician seeks to work on."

Read it on the official source

EPA keeps a list of these programs on its certification programs page.

Online or in person

Some programs offer remote or online testing.

EPA's page says: "Some programs offer remote or online testing that does not require you to travel to a physical testing location."

Read it on the official source

Ask the program before you pay how and when it gives the test.

What happens at the test

Type II and Type III tests must be closed-book, proctored, and given in a secure setting.

The federal rule says: "Technicians must pass a closed-book, proctored test, administered in a secure environment, by an EPA-approved certifying program to be certified as a Type II or Type III technician."

Read it on the official source

Proctors must check photo identification of each test taker.

The federal rule says: "Proctors are required to verify the identity of individuals taking the test by examining photo identification."

Read it on the official source

If you need the test read aloud to you, the rule has a way to ask for that.

A test may be given orally if the person asks in writing at least 30 days before the test date.

The federal rule says: "A test may be administered orally to any person who makes this request, in writing, to the program at least 30 days before the scheduled date for the examination."

Read it on the official source

Results and your card

The programs that give the test are also the ones that tell you the result and issue your card.

Programs must tell the test taker the result within 30 days of the test.

The federal rule says: "Certifying programs must inform the applicant of their test results no later than 30 days from the date of the test."

Read it on the official source

Programs must issue a wallet-sized ID card within 30 days of the test.

The federal rule says: "Certifying programs must issue a standard wallet-sized identification card no later than 30 days from the date of the test."

Read it on the official source

EPA does not require you to carry your certification card when servicing appliances.

EPA's page says: "EPA does not require you to carry your certification card with you when servicing appliances"

Read it on the official source

Your certification does not run out.

Section 608 certification does not expire.

EPA's page says: "Section 608 Technician Certification credentials do not expire."

Read it on the official source

How to prepare

Pick a program, then study the topic list for your part. Practise closed-book with questions that explain their answers. EPA does not look at preparation materials, ours included.

EPA does not review or approve any training or test-prep materials.

EPA's page says: "EPA does not review or approve any training preparatory programs or materials."

Read it on the official source
