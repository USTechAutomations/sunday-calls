Sunday Calls

Gift ideas for a grandmother who has everything

When someone already has what she needs, another object usually sits in a drawer. The ideas below are experiences and keepsakes instead of products, because those tend to get used and remembered rather than stored away.

Ideas worth considering

  • A framed photo she picked herself, not one you picked for her.
  • A recorded conversation about a memory she has mentioned before but never told in full.
  • A day trip back to a place that mattered to her, even if it is just across town.
  • A handwritten letter from every grandchild, kept together in one box.
  • An afternoon set aside for an actual visit, with nothing else on the calendar.
  • A tree, shrub, or perennial planted somewhere she will see it grow.
  • A cooking session where she teaches you the recipe nobody wrote down.
  • A quilt or blanket made from old family clothing.
  • A portrait sitting where she chooses her own outfit and pose.
  • A phone-call life story book, where someone else asks the questions and she only has to answer.
