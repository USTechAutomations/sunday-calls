A book of their life, told by phone

Sunday Calls is a pre-order for a printed book of a parent's or grandparent's life stories. An AI interviewer phones them once a week for eight weeks. You keep every recording and a printed hardcover book of about 100 pages. Photos are optional. The way photos are collected is not set up yet.

The storyteller does not type, install an app, or open email. They answer the phone.

How it works

Eight calls become recordings, and the recordings become the book.

Every call opens with: this is an AI interviewer calling for the family member, the call is recorded so we can write your book, is now a good time? The storyteller can say no and we never call again.

  1. Call one asks where they grew up and what they remember of that home.
  2. Call two asks about parents, grandparents, brothers, and sisters.
  3. Call three asks about school and the friends who stayed.
  4. Call four asks about first jobs and the work of their life.
  5. Call five asks about love, marriage, and the people they chose.
  6. Call six asks about children and the family they made, or about the family they kept if they did not raise children.
  7. Call seven asks about hard years and what helped.
  8. Call eight asks what they want the family to remember, and any advice they want written down.

These are the planned subjects. The interviewer follows what the storyteller actually says. A call can wander.
