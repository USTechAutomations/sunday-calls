Privacy and recordings

This is the whole rule we can state today. Gaps are marked in plain text.

  • An AI interviewer makes the calls. It is not a person.
  • Every call opens with: this is an AI interviewer calling for the family member, the call is recorded so we can write your book, is now a good time?
  • The storyteller can say no and we never call again.
  • The buyer confirms at checkout that the storyteller has agreed to receive the calls.
  • We never call a number nobody signed up.
  • No medical, legal or financial advice.
  • Recordings belong to the family. We delete recordings on request.
  • The thanks page can see a checkout reference in the address. It does not show that reference, and it does not show a name, phone number, or email.

A contact address for deletion requests is not published yet. Until that door exists, we cannot receive a deletion request. That is a gap, not a hidden mailbox.

What we do not do

  • We do not give medical, legal, or financial advice if a story comes to those subjects.
  • If a storyteller mentions health, money trouble, or harm, the interviewer still does not give advice. What we do beyond that is UNKNOWN until a written rule exists.
  • Whether a gift-giver's yes is enough under the phone rules is UNVERIFIED. The storyteller is still asked at the start of the call.
  • Some places require everyone on a call to agree before a recording. The interviewer says the call is recorded at the start of every call.
  • Sales tax on a printed book is UNKNOWN on this page.
