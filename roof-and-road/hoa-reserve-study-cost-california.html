When a firm is the right call

  • The three-year visual inspection. Where the replacement value of the major components is at least half the gross budget (not counting the reserve account), the law asks for a reasonably competent and diligent visual inspection at least once every three years.
  • The balcony and exterior stairway inspection under Civil Code 5551, which needs a licensed structural or civil engineer or an architect.
  • A lender, insurer or loan program that asks for a study by a named credential. We make no claim that any of them accepts a board-prepared plan.
  • A board that wants the protection of relying on outside advice. The Department of Real Estate's Reserve Study Guidelines (RE 25, August 2010, written before the 2014 renumbering of these laws) warns: Boards of Directors should consider the fact that they could lose the personal indemnity that comes from relying on professional advice if they choose to undertake their own study. Because of this, you should consider seeking legal advice before proceeding.

Between full studies, the law asks the board to review the study every year: The board shall review this study, or cause it to be reviewed, annually. That yearly review is the job Roof and Road is built for. The annual review and 2032 guide covers what it involves.
