Privacy

We never see your card or your association's numbers. Stripe, which takes the payment, keeps your email and payment details in our Stripe account; our own delivery records keep a scrambled copy of your email, used to match refunds, and nothing else about you. We send you no email ourselves; Stripe may send a receipt.

What the planner does with your numbers

The planner runs in your browser and sends nothing to us. Your numbers stay on your computer, in your browser or in the file you download with “Download my numbers”.

Your planner link

The address of your planner page is your key. Keep it private, and press “Save my link” when the planner first opens. If you lose it, write to ustechautomations@gmail.com with your receipt number.
