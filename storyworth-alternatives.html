Alternatives to StoryWorth when your parent will not type

StoryWorth sends weekly questions by text or email. Its site says stories can be shared by phone, by email, or on its website. The rows below are only what each company's own website said on 29 September 2026. Missing facts say UNKNOWN.

This page does not say which is better. It lists what each one says it is.

StoryWorth

  • Price: starting at $59.
  • How: weekly questions by text or email.
  • Printed book: one hardcover with the base plan; two color books with Unlimited.

Remento

  • Price: $99 for the first year. Renews at $99 a year or $12 a month.
  • How: prompts arrive by email or text message and the parent records a reply. Their site says no app or login is needed.
  • Printed book: one hardcover (up to 380 pages). Extra copies $69 each.

Storii

  • Price: check their site for today's price.
  • How: scheduled phone calls, up to 3 a week.
  • Books and files: a downloadable audiobook and a transcribed eBook PDF. Check their site for printed books.

Tell Mel

  • Price: Starter $39, Standard $129, or Premium $219 a year.
  • How: an AI interviewer phones the parent. Ten languages, including Spanish.
  • What you get: a written chapter after each call and a private website. Check their site for printed books.

Tell My Life Story

  • Price: from $149.
  • How: an AI interviewer calls the parent on any phone.
  • Printed book: hardcover. 3 to 5 printed copies included, depending on the plan.

HereAfter AI

  • We could not load their website on 29 September 2026. Check their site for price, format and printed books.

Meminto

  • Price: starting at $99, one-time.
  • How: in a web browser on a computer, tablet or phone, or in their iOS and Android apps.
  • Printed book: hardcover. Up to 100 pages on Essential, up to 300 on Complete.

Sunday Calls

  • Price: $99 pre-order. Regular price $199. Not a yearly price.
  • How: an AI interviewer phones once a week for eight weeks. No app and no email for the storyteller.
  • Printed book: one hardcover of about 100 pages, plus every recording. Photos are optional. The way photos are collected is not set up yet.
  • Timing: first books ship about 12 weeks after your storyteller's first call.
  • If we do not reach enough pre-orders to start, every pre-order is refunded in full.

We are not saying the other phone services are worse. We are saying what each format is, from their own sites, and what our pre-order is.

Rival prices and facts on this page were checked on their own websites on 29 September 2026 and may have changed.
