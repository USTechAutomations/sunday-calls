Alternatives to StoryWorth when your parent will not type

StoryWorth asks the parent to answer weekly email questions. The research note says the weak spot is that the parent has to write. The rows below are only what that note recorded. Missing facts say UNKNOWN.

The note's summary is that at least six other companies already sell this kind of product. This page does not add companies the note did not name.

StoryWorth

  • Price: $69, $109, or $199 a year.
  • How: answers weekly email questions. The parent writes.
  • Printed book: yes, 1–2 books.

Remento

  • Price: $99 a year.
  • How: records voice or video in an app. The parent does not type an email, but does use an app.
  • Printed book: yes, 1 hardcover. Extra copies $69.

Storii

  • Price: about $9.99 a month.
  • How: automated phone calls, up to 3 a week.
  • Printed book: not stated. The note says audiobook and PDF.

Tell Mel

  • Price: $39, $129, or $219 a year.
  • How: an AI interviewer phones the parent. 10 languages, including Spanish.
  • Printed book: not mentioned. The note says written chapters and a private website.

Tell My Life Story

  • Price: from $149.
  • How: AI phone interviews.
  • Printed book: hardcover, 20 copies included.

HereAfter AI

  • Price: from about $3.99 a month.
  • How: an AI interviewer in an app.
  • Printed book: no.

Meminto

  • Price: from about $99.
  • How: browser, app, or phone.
  • Printed book: yes. Page count is UNKNOWN.

Names with no price in the note

Afterlife AI, Memoirji, Memory Murals, Heritage Whisper, LifeStory, and Keepsake Project show up in the note as sites that write comparison articles. Their prices are UNKNOWN here, except Tell Mel, which is listed above.

Sunday Calls

  • Price: $99 pre-order. Regular price $199. Not a yearly price.
  • How: an AI interviewer phones once a week for eight weeks. No app and no email for the storyteller.
  • Printed book: one hardcover of about 100 pages, plus every recording. Photos are optional. The way photos are collected is not set up yet.
  • Timing: first books ship about 12 weeks after your storyteller's first call.
  • If we do not reach enough pre-orders to start, every pre-order is refunded in full.

We are not saying the other phone services are worse. We are saying what each format is, from the note, and what our pre-order is.
