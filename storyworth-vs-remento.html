How to read the table

Both companies send a weekly prompt to the storyteller. StoryWorth's site names a phone route among its ways to share, and Remento's site says no app or login is needed. What each one asks of your parent week to week is worth checking on their sites, since plans and terms change.

Price is not the same thing as value for your family. A $59 plan and a $99 plan can suit different storytellers, so think about who will answer, how long, and whether a bigger book matters to you.

A third way: weekly phone interviews

Sunday Calls works differently from both. An AI interviewer, not a human, phones your parent or grandparent once a week for eight weeks. There is nothing to type, no app to install, and no email to open. They answer the phone and talk.

  • Price: $99 pre-order, regular price $199. It is not a yearly price.
  • Eight weekly calls, each on a planned theme such as childhood, work, love, and hard years.
  • You keep every recording, plus one printed hardcover of about 100 pages.
  • Every call opens by saying it is an AI interviewer, that the call is recorded, and asking if now is a good time. Your parent can say no.

Other services also interview by phone, so this is one option among several. Sunday Calls is a $99 pre-order; the terms are on the home page.
